## 🎯 Change Management Strategy

### Change Readiness Assessment

#### Team Readiness Indicators

- **High Readiness**: Proactive questions, early adoption
- **Medium Readiness**: Neutral participation, follows guidance
- **Low Readiness**: Resistance, minimal engagement

#### Intervention Strategies

- **High Readiness**: Leverage as champions and mentors
- **Medium Readiness**: Provide additional support and examples
- **Low Readiness**: One-on-one coaching, address concerns directly

### Resistance Management

#### Common Resistance Sources

1. **Time Concerns**: "Too busy to learn new system"
2. **Complexity Fears**: "Looks too complicated"
3. **Value Skepticism**: "Current process works fine"
4. **Change Fatigue**: "Another new tool to learn"

#### Resistance Response Strategies

##### For Time Concerns

- Emphasize 60% time savings after adoption
- Provide quick-start guides (5-minute setup)
- Offer flexible training schedules

##### For Complexity Fears

- Start with simplest use cases
- Provide step-by-step walkthroughs
- Assign buddy system with experienced users

##### For Value Skepticism

- Share concrete ROI examples
- Demonstrate immediate benefits
- Connect to personal pain points

##### For Change Fatigue

- Acknowledge previous changes
- Emphasize "last major documentation change"
- Focus on long-term stability benefits

## 🚨 Crisis Communication Plan

### Crisis Levels

#### Level 1: Minor Issues

- **Definition**: Individual user problems, minor delays
- **Response Time**: 4 hours
- **Communication**: Slack channel update
- **Escalation**: If unresolved in 8 hours

#### Level 2: Moderate Issues

- **Definition**: Team-wide problems, phase delays
- **Response Time**: 2 hours
- **Communication**: Slack + email to affected teams
- **Escalation**: If unresolved in 4 hours

#### Level 3: Critical Issues

- **Definition**: System-wide failures, rollout stoppage
- **Response Time**: 30 minutes
- **Communication**: All channels + emergency notifications
